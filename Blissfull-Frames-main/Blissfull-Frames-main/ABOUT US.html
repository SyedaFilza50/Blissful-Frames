About Us

How We Started

Our journey in transforming living spaces began with a passion for creating harmonious and inviting environments. Founded in 2010, our company started as a small team of visionary designers united by a love for aesthetics and functionality. Our founder, Laiba Nisar, envisioned a world where every living room could be a sanctuary of comfort and style. With dedication, creativity, and a keen eye for detail, we embarked on a mission to help homeowners reimagine their living spaces. Over the years, we've grown into a renowned interior design firm, known for our personalized approach and innovative designs. Today, we continue to blend contemporary trends with timeless elegance, crafting living rooms that reflect the unique personalities and lifestyles of our clients.



Our Vision

At Blissful Frames, our vision is to redefine the essence of living room design by creating spaces that inspire and rejuvenate. We believe that a living room should be more than just a place to relax; it should be a reflection of one's personality, a hub of creativity, and a sanctuary of comfort. Our goal is to blend functionality with aesthetic brilliance, ensuring each design is not only visually stunning but also tailored to enhance the everyday lives of our clients. We are committed to pushing the boundaries of traditional design, embracing innovation, and constantly evolving to set new standards in the world of interior design. With every project, we strive to transform living rooms into beautiful, functional, and timeless spaces where memories are made and cherished.



Our Mission

At BlissFul Frames, our mission is to create living rooms that embody comfort, style, and functionality. We believe that the living room is the heart of the home, a place where memories are made and cherished. Our goal is to transform these spaces into personalized sanctuaries that reflect the unique tastes and lifestyles of our clients. By combining innovative design with a commitment to quality and sustainability, we strive to deliver spaces that inspire and delight. We are dedicated to exceeding expectations and making every living room a true reflection of those who live in it.
